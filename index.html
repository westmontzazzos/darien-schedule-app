SCHEDULING RULES
Official rules enforced by the schedule checker
🕐 Store Hours
Open 9AM daily
Sun–Thu: Close 11PM
Fri–Sat: Close Midnight
🍕 Pizza & 🔪 Kitchen Rules
Kitchen & Pizza close 1 hour before store close (10PM Sun–Thu, 11PM Fri–Sat)
Each section: opener by 9AM · 1 closer (C) required, staying until Kitchen/Pizza close
Minimum working 4–8PM: Mon–Thu 2 Pizza / 2 Kitchen · Fri 6 Pizza / 3 Kitchen · Sat 5 Pizza / 3 Kitchen · Sun 4 Pizza / 2 Kitchen
Baseline coverage: at least 1 Pizza + 1 Kitchen person on at all times the store is open — no gaps
🍽️ Dish Rules
1 dish person covering 4PM until store close, every day
Must have a closer (C)
👔 Management Rules
1 opening manager (by 9AM) · 1 closing manager (variable start, stays until store close)
Scheduled before 9AM is flagged as informational only — not a violation (e.g. early call-in for a large order)
🍸 Bartender Rules
Opener: 9AM–5PM
Closer: 5PM until store close, marked 5-C
🍽️ Server Rules
2 servers, both starting between 4–5PM
Non-closer: out at 8PM · Closer (C): stays until store close
🪑 Host Rules
Fri & Sat only, 4PM–8PM
💵 Cashier Rules
Sun: 3 cashiers · opener by 11:30AM (only day with an opener)
Fri: 3 cashiers, all in by 4PM  ·  Sat: 2 cashiers, all in by 4PM
Mon–Thu: 1 cashier, in by 4PM
No cashier starts later than 4PM, any day · everyone ends at 8PM regardless of start time
🚗 Driver Rules
Morning: Mon–Thu 1 driver by 10:30AM · Fri–Sun 2 drivers by 10:30AM
1 closer (C) required, every day
Mon–Thu: at least 3 drivers at night — 2nd in by 4PM, all 3 in by 5:30PM
Fri: at least 6 drivers — 2nd in by 4PM, all in by 5PM
Sat: at least 5 drivers — 2nd in by 4PM, all in by 5PM
Sun: at least 5 drivers (2 morning drivers hold over) — 3rd in by 4PM, all 5 in by 5PM
⚠️ Override Policy
If a rule cannot be followed, tap ⚠️ OVERRIDE RULE on the error.
You will be required to enter your PIN and acknowledge the rule being broken.
Overrides suppress the error for that week only.
